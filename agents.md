# Agents

## Scout

Finds symbolic leads and writes raw lead cards.

## Translator

Turns symbolic phrases into candidate mathematical interpretations.

## Experimenter

Runs computational tests and writes result JSON.

## Skeptic

Designs null models and tries to kill patterns.

## Conjecturer

Promotes surviving interpretations into precise conjectures.

## Lean Porter

Exports conjectures to Lean theorem skeletons.

## Proof Agent

Attempts to remove `sorry` and produce compiling Lean proofs.

## Archivist

Maintains indexes, links, reports, and status labels.

## Evolution Agent

Updates `evolution.md` when a methodological lesson changes future search.
