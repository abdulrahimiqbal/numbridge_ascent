# L-0003: Prime-gap root rhythm

## Symbolic intuition

Prime gaps may have a digital-root rhythm.

## Candidate formalizations

1. `digital_root_10(p_{n+1} - p_n)` has a nonrandom distribution.
2. The sequence of gap roots has autocorrelation.
3. The root sequence differs from wheel-sieved pseudo-prime gaps.

## Current result

Weak. Initial effects are largely explained by parity, small-prime constraints, and the distribution of small gaps.
