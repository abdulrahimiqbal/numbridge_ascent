# L-0004: 7 resists factor collapse

## Symbolic intuition

The symbol 7 may encode resistance to factor collapse.

## Candidate formalizations

1. Numbers with decimal digital root 7 have fewer prime factors.
2. Numbers ending in 7 have higher prime likelihood after controls.
3. Numbers that reach 7 under multiplicative persistence have unusual structure.
4. Residue class 7 modulo some `m` has lower divisor complexity.
5. In base 7, collapse should be interpreted via digit-sum modulo 6.

## Current result

Active. The first two interpretations are likely weak after residue controls. Multiplicative persistence remains a more interesting candidate.
