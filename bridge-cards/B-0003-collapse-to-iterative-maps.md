# B-0003: Collapse to iterative maps

## Symbolic form

Numbers collapse into roots, states, or essences.

## Mathematical form

An iterated function on integers or finite states.

Examples:

- repeated digit sum
- multiplicative persistence
- aliquot sequences
- factorization trees
- residue dynamics
